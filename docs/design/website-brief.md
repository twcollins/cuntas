# Cúntas: website brief

Oct 24, 2026 · @Tom

## What Cúntas is

Cúntas is a household money app that tells you the next sensible step with your money, using the tax rules where you live, and keeps your data on your device.

**One-line pitch:** Your budget, your tax and your next financial step in one place, with your data kept on your own device.

**Tagline options**

- Know your next money move.
- Budgeting that knows the tax rules where you live.
- Your money, your device, your plan.

**Who it's for**

- Households in Ireland and the UK who want one view of accounts, bills, debts, pensions and tax.
- People who don't want a bank login or their spending history on someone else's server.
- Couples and families who share some money, and cross-border households (living in one country, earning in the other).
- People who are self-employed or paid by payslip and want to check their tax.

"Cúntas" is Irish for "account" (said roughly *KOON-tus*). The site should say this once, near the top.

## Why it's different

Four points carry the homepage. Each could be a hero card.

1. **Your data stays on your device.** Everything is stored in your browser or phone. You don't need an account, and there's no bank login unless you choose one.
2. **It tells you what to do next.** A step-by-step plan works through your situation in order: track spending, build a starter emergency fund, clear expensive debt, take any employer pension match, and so on. It shows the one step that matters now and why.
3. **The tax rules are built in.** Irish and UK tax for this year and next: take-home pay, payslip checks, self-employed tax estimates, pension allowances and Scottish income tax. Every figure links to its official source.
4. **Guidance, not advice.** Cúntas explains the general rules and your numbers. It never names or sells financial products and doesn't take commission.

## How it works

Getting started takes three steps and doesn't need an account.

1. **Start a household.** Add the people in it, their pay and where they live. Or open the demo household to look around first.
2. **Bring in your accounts.** Download a statement file from your bank and import it, or add balances by hand. Re-importing the same file is safe.
3. **Follow your next step.** The Overview shows where you are in the plan, what to do now and the numbers behind it. The Guide explains each screen.

## Privacy and your data

Your financial data lives on your device and nowhere else, unless you turn on sync.

- **On your device:** households, transactions, budgets and tax details are stored in your browser or phone. No account is needed, and there are no ads or tracking.
- **Leaves the device only when you ask:** exchange rates are downloaded from the European Central Bank (ECB). Sync, sharing and bank connections are optional and need a Cúntas server you choose.
- **Yours to take or delete:** export everything at any time as JSON or CSV, or as a passphrase-encrypted backup. One button deletes everything on the device.
- **No identity numbers stored:** Cúntas doesn't ask for or keep your PPSN or National Insurance number.

The flip side to say plainly: if you clear your browser or lose your phone without a backup, the data is gone. The site should tell people to make a backup.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?** No. You only need a sign-in to sync or share a household, and that uses a passkey, with no password.

**Can you see my money?** No. Your data is on your device. We don't run a server that holds it unless you choose to sync.

**Does it connect to my bank?** You import the statement files your bank already lets you download. Live bank connections are optional and need a Cúntas server.

**Is this financial advice?** No. Cúntas explains the general rules and works out your numbers. For advice on your own situation, talk to a qualified adviser.

**How accurate are the tax figures?** Each figure comes from Revenue, HMRC or the relevant official source, and the app links to it. Next year's figures are provisional until they become law.

**Can my partner and I use it together?** Yes. Share a household through a Cúntas server and each of you sees the same plan on your own device.

**Is there a phone app?** Cúntas works in your phone's browser and can be added to your home screen. App Store and Google Play versions are being designed.

**What happens if I lose my phone?** Restore from your last backup or export. Without one, the data can't be recovered, because no copy exists anywhere else.

**Claims to avoid**

- Don't call it financial advice, or say it will save people a set amount of money.
- Don't name banks or products as partners or recommendations. Naming banks whose files import is fine.
- Don't promise App Store or Google Play availability, US support or a launch date until they're decided.
- Don't say "end-to-end encrypted". Sync is encrypted on the server, which is different.
- Don't say "free forever" until pricing is decided.
- Screenshots use the demo household only, never real data.

**Open questions**

- Pricing, and whether a hosted sync server will be offered.
- Domain, logo and who publishes the site.
